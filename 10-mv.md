## Purpose:
The mv command moves files or directories to another location or changes their names.

## Syntax:
mv old_folder new_folder

## Examples:
# Rename a file:
mv notes.txt linux-notes.txt
## Move a file into a directory:
mv linux-notes.txt backup/

## Practice:
touch test.txt
mv test.txt new-test.txt
ls

## Real-Time Use:
Useful for organising files, renaming reports, and moving logs into appropriate directories.
